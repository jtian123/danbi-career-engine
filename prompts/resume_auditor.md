# Resume source and editorial audit

Public fixtures are synthetic examples, not verified applicant claims. For a real private build, review each rendered claim and course label against its cited evidence. Review dates, titles, skill levels, scope, metrics, JD relevance and redundancy. Course availability does not establish personal attainment. A plan or reviewer cannot create a new fact. Record unsupported claims and genuine gaps; pass only the exact current content after revision and final page fitting.
